TooLove

Airbrush Art & Stencil Tools

by Silke May

TooLove combines analog airbrush art with small digital stencil tools. The tools create cuttable stencil SVGs with automatic slots from images and text for plotters, airbrush, spray art and graphic stencil workflows. The macOS applications support both Intel Macs and Apple Silicon systems. The iOS versions are available on the Apple App Store.

LabeLove – Image to Stencil Text with Slots (macOS)

Input
Output with Slots

LabeLove converts text images into cuttable stencil SVGs with automatic slots. It is designed for lettering, logos and words that need to stay connected when cut. The macOS version is available for both Intel Macs and Apple Silicon Macs.

Download for macOS

SobeLover – Graphic Edge Art from Images (macOS)

Input
Output

SobeLover creates bold, high-contrast edge visuals from images. It is useful for graphic experiments, stencil preparation and strong spray-art style effects. The macOS version is available for both Intel Macs and Apple Silicon Macs.

Download for macOS

StenciLover – Cuttable Stencil Designs from Images (macOS)

Input
Output with Marks

StenciLover turns images into bold, simplified stencil designs. The goal is not photorealism, but clean, plakative and cuttable stencil artwork for real use. The macOS version creates cuttable SVG stencil designs and is available for both Intel Macs and Apple Silicon Macs.

Download for macOS

LabeLove – Text Stencil Tool (iOS)

LabeLove for iOS is a mobile text stencil tool. Unlike the macOS version, it works with typed text and creates stencil previews with slots. The iOS version runs on iPhone and is available on the Apple App Store.

Download for iOS

StenciLove – Multi-Layer Stencil App (iOS)

StenciLove for iOS creates bold multi-layer stencil designs from photos. It is made for quick mobile stencil drafts and graphic image reduction. The iOS version runs on iPhone and is available on the Apple App Store.

Download for iOS

ChromaLove – Color Stencil App (iOS)

ChromaLove for iOS is a color-based stencil tool for creating bold graphic stencil drafts from images. Includes automatic stencil slots and registration marks for real cuttable stencil workflows. It focuses on color reduction, clear visual layers and quick mobile experiments for stencil and spray-art workflows. The iOS version runs on iPhone and is available on the Apple App Store.
