My work combines airbrush painting, acrylics and stencil techniques. All of the stencils used in these works are created from photographs and designed specifically for each individual painting. Looking for better ways to turn photographs into practical, cuttable stencils for use with a plotter eventually led me to develop my own digital stencil tools.

Original Character · Moving Image

BirdieMan

From Airbrush Character to Moving Image

BirdieMan started with a simple problem: I wanted a comic character of my own – one that I could freely use in my artwork and stories. So I created one.

From an airbrush character came a story, from the story came animation and AI-generated video – and one experiment gradually led to the next. BirdieMan has become an ongoing project combining character design, visual storytelling, airbrush art and experimental moving images.

BirdieMan – First Story · Experimental short film · 2026

Tools I Built Along the Way

Sometimes making art requires a tool that simply doesn't exist. These small macOS and iOS applications grew out of my own creative workflow – from stencil design and graphic image reduction to sequence-based video work.

Video Tool · macOS

PaneLove – Video Sequence Assembly

Frame-precise sequence preview
Sequence and trim workspace

PaneLove is a lightweight macOS tool for reviewing, frame-precise trimming, arranging and joining matching video clips into a single MP4. It is designed especially for sequence-based and AI-generated video workflows.

Apple Silicon · Signed & notarized · FFmpeg included · MP4 output

Stencil Tools

Stencil & Graphic Tools

Small creative applications for artists working with airbrush, stencil art, plotters and graphic image reduction. The tools help create practical stencil designs, cuttable SVG files, automatic stencil slots and simplified graphic artwork.

LabeLove – Text Stencil Tool for macOS

Input
Output with Slots

LabeLove converts text images into cuttable stencil SVG files with automatic slots. It is designed for lettering, logos and words that need to stay connected when cut with a plotter or used as an airbrush stencil.

Download for macOS

SobeLover – Graphic Edge Art from Images

Input
Output

SobeLover creates bold, high-contrast edge visuals from images. It is made for graphic experiments, stencil preparation and strong spray-art style image reduction.

Download for macOS

StenciLover – Cuttable Stencil Designs from Images

Input
Output with Marks

StenciLover turns images into bold, simplified and cuttable stencil designs. It focuses on practical SVG stencil artwork for real-world airbrush, spray-art and plotter workflows.

Download for macOS
Mobile Stencil Tools

Stencil Tools for iPhone

LabeLove – Text Stencil Tool

LabeLove for iPhone creates stencil previews with automatic slots directly from typed text – useful for quick lettering and stencil design experiments on the go.

Download for iOS

StenciLove – Multi-Layer Stencil App

StenciLove creates bold multi-layer stencil designs from photos. It is designed for quick mobile stencil drafts and graphic image reduction.

Download for iOS

ChromaLove – Color Stencil App

ChromaLove is a color-based stencil tool for creating bold graphic stencil drafts from images. It combines color reduction with automatic stencil slots and registration markers for practical multi-layer stencil workflows.

Download for iOS

About

Silke May is a Berlin-based artist working with airbrush, acrylics and stencil techniques.

TooLove is the place where these works – and the experiments that grow out of them – come together. Digital tools, animation and small software projects often begin with a practical problem encountered while making art.

If the tool doesn't exist, making one is sometimes simply part of the process.
